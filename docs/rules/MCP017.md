# MCP017: Approval removed

**Status:** Legacy v0.1 diff finding; represented by `approval_removed` changes in v0.2.

**Severity:** High

## What it detects

A tool that required approval at the Git baseline but no longer has a recognized approval decorator or rejecting guard. The v0.2 diff contract reports this as a structured change rather than a synthetic SARIF finding.

## Why it matters

Consequential operations may execute without the human boundary reviewers previously approved.

## Vulnerable example

```text
requires_approval: true -> false
```

## Safe example

Approval remains enabled, or a missing approval boundary is added.

## Remediation

Restore approval enforcement or document a time-bounded reviewed exception.

## Limitations

Ruleset 0.1 recognizes decorator keywords and local approval guards. Approval enforced entirely outside the scanned source may require a documented suppression.
